# kv.vertical-band-corridor: Portrait key visual: one-point corridor, red band with a vertical title, collage objects, copy block on paper

> Measured basis. Slot budgets, parameter ranges and palette are authoritative in `manifest.json`; the style pack (`styles/shinbo/style.json`) owns the font roles, grammar, materials, motion grammar and checks.

Canvas 1080x1920 (1080x1920, 1440x2560, 720x1280). Source: Nocturne Corridor poster first pass and final (samples poster).

## When to use
Poster or key visual of a fictional work in the style: architecture top, title band right, copy bottom left, collage around but never over copy.

## Measured
- first pass: a key sticker over the tagline and the reading (2 covered); 2 contrast failures on the screentone band
- final: key moved to the corridor, paper plates under the credits; passes

## Relations vs decoration
- **Relation**: Copy block on paper bottom left on one shared left line; the title column lives in the red band.
- **Relation**: Collage objects frame the corridor and never cover copy (browser rule collage-cover).
- **Decoration**: Screentone band at the foot with paper plates under the credits.
- **Decoration**: Cut-paper butterfly.
