# card.bracket-quote: Quote card: 『 』 brackets in red at twice the text size, one centred sentence

> Measured basis. Slot budgets, parameter ranges and palette are authoritative in `manifest.json`; the style pack (`styles/shinbo/style.json`) owns the font roles, grammar, materials, motion grammar and checks.

Canvas 1920x1080 (1920x1080, 1280x720, 3840x2160). Source: Nocturne Corridor c05 final (samples c05-white-bracket).

## When to use
One quoted sentence as a full-screen beat.

## Slots
| Slot | Kind (font role) | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| open | text (display) | <= 1, bleeds, decoration |
| close | text (display) | <= 1, bleeds, decoration |
| quote | text (title) | <= 10 |
| label | text (label) | <= 4 |

## Measured
- brackets 300px red; sentence 136px centred at 0.435H; final moved the brackets out to 70px from the edges

## Relations vs decoration
- **Relation**: Brackets sit diagonally outside the measured ends of the sentence (open above-left, close below-right), about twice its size; they move with the copy.
- **Decoration**: Brackets are decoration (exempt from overlap and contrast).
