# Concepts

1. **Held Alice, the song moves around her.** (chosen) A Vocaloid-style illustration of Alice holds
   the frame like Vampire's Miku while the lyrics, graphics and OpenAlice's interface move on the
   160 BPM grid. Each section uses the device the references use there: clusters in the verse,
   strips and re-crops in the pre-chorus, slams, tickers and a split screen in the chorus, a
   silhouette in the bridge, a duplication grid and strobing beat panel at the peak, a text ring
   at the end. Carrier: Alice herself; hard cuts only into the chorus, bridge and final chorus.
   Tools: generate.cjs (ChatGPT image generation), HTML/GSAP, Strudel score.
2. **Wallpaper stills cut to music.** Several generated key-visual stills of Alice, one per
   section, cut on downbeats. Rejected by the user on the first attempt: good wallpapers, not a
   MAD. It had no grammar from the genre; the pictures changed, but nothing moved on the beat.
3. **Interface lyric video.** OpenAlice screens as the background with lyrics over them. Rejected:
   a lyric slideshow; the character, which is what makes a Miku-style MAD, would be missing.

Why 1: it is built from the measured grammar of seven real MVs, puts the character at the centre
the way the genre does, and still lets OpenAlice's own interface into the film through the beat
panel and the chibi grid.
