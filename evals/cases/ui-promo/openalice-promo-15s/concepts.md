# Concepts

1. **One question, three screens, no reset.** (chosen) A question typed into Ask Alice cuts to the
   same words in the agent's terminal; the agent's red +9.1% cuts to the same number in the Inbox
   report; the report's AAPL cuts to the AAPL position in the portfolio; the portfolio becomes a
   card beside the logo. Carrier: one fact per cut. Look: OpenAlice's own dark UI, 2x captures,
   short captions. Tools: capture.cjs, HTML/GSAP camera, Strudel score.
2. **Feature tour.** Home, workspace, Inbox, portfolio, market, each with a caption. Rejected: the
   slideshow the design-pipeline gates exist to stop; it shows pages, not the product doing work.
3. **Terminal only.** Stay inside the agent's session for 15 s. Rejected: it hides the Inbox and
   the portfolio, which are what make OpenAlice more than a chat.

Why 1: it proves the product claim (research in a workspace, findings in the Inbox, next to your
portfolio) with the product's own data, and the match cuts make the same fact visibly travel.
