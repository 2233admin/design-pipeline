# Concepts

1. **The film catches its own slideshow.** (chosen) One `next` call scaffolds five frames, a
   generic five-card draft fills them, `film check` stops it with `slideshow-handoffs` and a fix,
   the fix carries one green dot through every frame until the check passes, the same gate
   sweeps two more model lanes, and everything collapses into the logo.
   Carrier: the green rule, then the strip, then the dot; the camera never resets except for
   the one cut on the drop. Look: DESIGN.md status colours on near-black, monospace for
   commands, one saturated off-brand colour for the generic draft. Tools: HTML/GSAP
   composition, Strudel score. No missing licenses.
2. **Three models, one bar.** Three lanes draft side by side and one gate line filters them.
   Rejected as the central idea: abstract, and it shows no fix. Kept as the 15.5-17.5 s beat.
3. **Blueprint first.** DESIGN.md and MOTION.md unfold, then a UI builds from them (the README's
   "draw the blueprint before building"). Rejected: documents on screen are static, and the
   product's newest proof is the film gates.

Why 1: the product exists to stop exactly this draft, so showing the stop and the fix is the
most honest demonstration, and the generic draft makes the payoff visible by contrast.
