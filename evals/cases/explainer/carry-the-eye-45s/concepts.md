# Concepts

1. **Make the eye visible.** (chosen) A yellow gaze marker shows where the viewer looks. In a
   slideshow it has to hunt after every cut and leaves red jumps; with carries it rides one subject
   and leaves one green path. The film carries its own boundaries with the same marker, dot and
   path. Carrier: the gaze marker, then the dot, then the path. Look: dark stage, one screen,
   status colours, monospace headline. Tools: HTML/GSAP composition, Strudel score.
2. **Split screen from the start.** Bad and good versions side by side for 45 s. Rejected: the
   viewer has nothing to compare against until the rule is named, and two screens halve the size of
   every demonstration.
3. **Kinetic-type lecture.** The rule and its four forms as animated text. Rejected: telling
   instead of showing, and nothing on screen carries anything.

Why 1: the cost of a cut is invisible in a normal film; the gaze marker makes it visible, so the
rule reads as a fix for something the viewer has just watched go wrong.
