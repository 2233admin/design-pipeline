# ascii-product-film Specification

## Purpose
Define the local ASCII canvas launch film for SeedController: black-and-white ASCII imagery, restrained launch pacing and product claims sourced from the SeedController README and CONTEXT, presented as an illustrative film without provider calls or publication.

## Requirements

### Requirement: A seekable ASCII product-film study

The experiment SHALL provide a local 40-second HTML film using ASCII structure and illustrative
product imagery, with a finite timeline, playback controls and declared asset provenance.

#### Scenario: A viewer reviews the study
- **WHEN** the viewer opens the local preview at port 4174
- **THEN** playback and chapter seeking show the planned sequence
- **AND** the documentation identifies this as a silent concept rather than a live generation
  recording or an accepted final promotional direction.
