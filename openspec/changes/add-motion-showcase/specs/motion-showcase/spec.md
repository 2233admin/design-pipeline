## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Direct visual motion demonstration

The experiment SHALL provide a locally viewable HTML page with orbital motion, kinetic type,
scroll reveals and a pointer-responsive poster, with recorded design and motion foundations.

#### Scenario: A viewer explores the showcase
- **WHEN** the viewer opens the local preview at port 4173
- **THEN** the page demonstrates visible motion with a pause control and reduced-motion behavior
- **AND** desktop and narrow-layout observations are recorded separately from validator tests.
