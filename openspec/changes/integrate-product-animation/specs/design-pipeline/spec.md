## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Product-animation integration proof

A product-animation integration SHALL preserve product-launch-video through Stage 0 and toolchain resolution, provide one finite paused seekable timeline, and deliver an encoded complete film.

#### Scenario: Persistent research provenance

- **WHEN** an excerpt enters the single research document
- **THEN** the local addition SHALL retain its concrete source attribution.
- **AND** saving SHALL leave the original workspace research and saved-version thumbnails visible.
- **AND** Inbox SHALL receive a report/attachment reference associated with the original Session, not move the original file.

#### Scenario: Sample before expansion

- **WHEN** the 7.5-second sample plays once at normal speed with chapter narration hidden
- **THEN** the main reviewer SHALL identify the added content, original location, retained version and Inbox reference.
- **AND** ambiguity or any trade interpretation SHALL block expansion until rework.

#### Scenario: Autonomous complete-film playback

- **WHEN** the full film plays without scrolling or clicking
- **THEN** its finite timeline SHALL reach Ready for your review and No trade placed.
- **AND** no trade proposal, approval or transaction SHALL be depicted.
- **AND** metadata, full decode, deterministic seek, pause/replay and reduced-motion controls SHALL be verified against the declared duration rather than old 24-second assertions.

#### Scenario: Honest evidence and preservation

- **WHEN** redesign outputs are rendered
- **THEN** the rejected old film and evidence SHALL already be preserved separately.
- **AND** old receipts SHALL not be reused as current evidence, toolchain blocked SHALL remain blocked, and user-owned osprey work SHALL remain unchanged.
- **AND** the motion implementation SHALL declare semantic beats, shared anchors, renderer ownership and bounded response parameters in a project-owned authored graph.
- **AND** a graph track SHALL compile to the existing paused seekable timeline without depending on wall-clock time, unseeded randomness or input history.

#### Scenario: Evidence-lineage choreography

- **WHEN** the complete film is sampled at its declared beat boundaries
- **THEN** the original research SHALL remain the semantic source while the visual trace moves through SOURCE, RETAIN and REFERENCE anchors.
- **AND** retained versions and Inbox reference SHALL appear as connected states rather than replacing the source with separate full-screen panels.
- **AND** the technical comparison SHALL remain separate from user visual acceptance.
