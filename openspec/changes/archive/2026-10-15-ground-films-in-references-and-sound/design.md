# Design

Keep one authoritative contract in `product-film-direction.md`. Strengthen its front-door pointer
so the agent reaches reference and sound work before it selects a template, visual grammar or
runtime. Expand the existing eval manifest with ordinary-language and existing-footage scenarios.
These describe desired agent behavior; validation of the manifest only checks their structure and
routing, not whether an agent has demonstrated creative quality on those scenarios.

Record source observations separately from an adaptation proposal. The SEAM video and its read-only
workflow/storyboard can establish visible footage and compositing traits. Browser metadata proves
duration and dimensions, not musical genre, BPM, track provenance or an audible listening review.

The earlier preview stays intact for traceability. Its QA record gains the user's rejection.
The next film's style must follow inspected moving references and available footage, with music
and sound planned together with editing. A single-take node animation is one option, not the default
answer to all promotional briefs.
