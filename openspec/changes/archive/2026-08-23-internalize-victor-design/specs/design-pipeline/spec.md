### Requirement: Visual form is grounded before direction selection

Before selecting a visual direction, the pipeline SHALL identify the deliverable form, the reader
action, and whether the proposed carrier can perform that action on its own. When the brief implies
a sequence, set, or state flow, the pipeline SHALL NOT compress it to a single canvas for production
convenience.

#### Scenario: A brief implies a sequence

- **WHEN** a brief describes a tutorial, case, pitch, multi-page set, or related product states
- **THEN** the direction work records a sequence/state carrier or records an explicit unresolved
  form risk before production
- **AND** a style label alone cannot release the direction

### Requirement: Visual techniques carry a reason

Each major visual technique in a direction or review SHALL name a subject/reference cause and its
intended viewer effect. A technique that only signals that the output is designed is treated as
unjustified.

#### Scenario: A direction uses a framing device

- **WHEN** a direction adds a line, block, icon, texture, effect, material treatment, or motion
- **THEN** the direction explains what caused it and what it helps the viewer understand, feel, or do

### Requirement: Craft review checks both excess and absence

When anti-template review is active, it SHALL check both generic AI-formula patterns and
over-restraint. A title over an untreated asset with weak decorative furniture SHALL NOT pass as a
finished direction without an explicit, product-grounded exception.

### Requirement: Decision copy remains source-shaped

Issue, PR, interface, and other human decision artifacts SHALL distinguish sourced facts, real voice,
and structural copy. They SHALL preserve scope and uncertainty and SHALL NOT invent metrics,
metadata, product behavior, quotes, or personal experience to make the artifact sound designed.

#### Scenario: Evidence is incomplete

- **WHEN** a finding lacks a reproduction detail or confirmation
- **THEN** the artifact names the missing input and marks the point as pending or unverified
- **AND** it does not fill the gap with plausible narrative
