# OpenAlice product film

## Product Context
Subject: OpenAlice, a local-first trading research orchestrator. Audience: agent-using researchers. Job: understand how existing agents, research history, and human approval connect within 24 seconds. This is an original schematic film, not footage of the real application.

## Overview
Signature: a horizontal Git trail accumulates five narrative beats and ends at a visibly closed approval boundary. Dense product details appear one at a time in a large editorial composition. The film advances autonomously; the illustrated trade does not.

## Colors
Background #0a0d12, panels #111721, foreground #eef2f8, supporting text #a9b6c9, blue #7aa2d8 for research, mint #81d6b3 for linked artifacts, amber #e8b64c exclusively for the human boundary. Never encode meaning through color alone.

## Typography
Segoe UI/system sans; Consolas monospace metadata. Headlines 62–88px, feature captions 28px, product rows 22–26px, persistent labels 16–18px at 1280×720. Short English film copy avoids overloading the reading holds. No remote fonts.

## Layout
Fixed 16:9 output with 64px horizontal title safe area. The preview scales the entire composition proportionally; it does not reflow or scroll the film. Editorial left titles and right product schematics give way to the centered final boundary. Separate responsive preview controls remain outside the captured frame.

## Components
Original agent routing diagram, Git commit list, scheduled task row, closed approval boundary, five-part progress spine, and semantic preview buttons. Diagrams describe product concepts and are labelled illustrative. No dependency on the old scroll page.

## Do's and Don'ts
Do hold readable scenes for at least two seconds. Do label the schematic and beta execution. Do keep the pending decision closed. Don't show prices, profits, or real transactions. Don't imply actual product UI fidelity. Don't use a scroll input as a clock.

## Source Decisions
Adopted: product facts already documented in the user-owned OpenAlice showcase foundation (feature names, local-first storage, Git history, human approval). Rejected: its webpage-only layout and ban on timed scene advancement, which apply to that separate target. No OpenAlice source, imagery, screenshots, or branding assets are copied. HyperFrames 0.8.46 and GSAP 3.15.0 are isolated target dependencies, explicitly needed for the requested video capability.
