# Field Notes

## Product Context
SeedController organizes prompts, references and image/video outputs in a node canvas. Agent can
generate and modify plans. Existing showcase videos supply authentic project material for this concept.

## Overview
A 32-second film opens on a finished moving world, studies its details, reveals connected creative
work on the canvas, and closes on the product. The SEAM reference informs material and compositing.

## Colors
Cool charcoal #090d11, silver #b9c5cf, bone #e6e9e7. Blue footage retains restrained chroma;
the luminous moon supplies a warm/cool contrast. Canvas relationships emerge on pale gray.

## Typography
System sans for short Chinese titles; condensed-looking narrow tracking for the English brand;
Consolas for small analytical annotations. Sparse copy with generous film title-safe margins.

## Layout
1600x900 fixed composition within a responsive 16:9 surface. Full-frame footage, asymmetric crop
inserts, restrained connection graphics, a moving node composition, and a held brand close.

## Components
One video-composite canvas, media pool, original stereo score, explicit play-with-sound action,
play/pause, seek, mute, volume, fullscreen and replay. Transcript and asset notes outside the film.

## Do's and Don'ts
Let actual footage carry texture and motion. Keep overlays subordinate to the image and meaningful
to the product. Do not claim concept graphics are a real generation session or copied product UI.

## Source Decisions
Adopted: project-owned city/night/moon showcase videos and SEAM's observed compositing traits.
Rejected: copying reference footage/audio, repeating static scene cards, and unrelated anime imagery.
