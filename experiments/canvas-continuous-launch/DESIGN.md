# SeedController / Connected imagination

## Product Context
SeedController is a node-based image/video creation workspace. Reference materials, prompts,
generation nodes, and results form a workflow; Agent can generate and modify canvas plans.
Sources: seedcontrol/README.md and seedcontrol/CONTEXT.md, inspected locally.

## Overview
Present the canvas through one unfolding action in a 22-second silent film. A cursor becomes a
prompt, its connection carries the camera into an ASCII image, color resolves the image, and
the same image expands into connected storyboard crops before folding into the brand mark.

## Colors
Near-black #08090b, white #f4f4f0, silver #989ca4 form the structural layer. Real image assets
introduce warm sandstone, apricot light, and turquoise water in the canvas and output scenes.
The image reveal becomes full color. Color follows
scene content rather than a blanket monochrome treatment. Avoid neon terminal styling.

## Typography
System sans for large Chinese headlines. Consolas / monospace for ASCII art and node labels.
Readable copy remains separate from character art and is represented in a static transcript.

## Layout
1600x900 composition scales uniformly inside a responsive 16:9 viewport. Controls and transcript
reflow outside the film. Key headlines stay near the center and within title-safe boundaries.

## Components
One continuous Canvas 2D world, shared prompt and image actors, character image field, linked
storyboard crops and a brand close. Accessible playback controls and a transcript live outside
the composition. No independent full-screen scene containers.

## Do's and Don'ts
Use short copy and one feature per beat. Keep the end card legible. Do not claim model speed,
invent live outputs, or present an illustrative graph as a pixel-accurate production UI capture.

## Source Decisions
Adopted: documented SeedController capabilities; original character art and typography.
Rejected: Apple assets or branding, external footage, and unverified performance claims.
