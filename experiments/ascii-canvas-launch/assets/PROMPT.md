## Prompt

Create a cinematic landscape still for a premium HTML promotional film for an AI creative canvas.
Use case: ads-marketing, original demonstration media. A spectacular aerial wide shot of an
otherworldly but photoreal coastal mountain range: massive layered burnt-orange sandstone cliffs
and dunes sweeping from lower left to the horizon, a sinuous turquoise-blue lagoon through the
center, a pale apricot sun low in mist, deep indigo mountain shadows, delicate sea haze.
Sophisticated natural color, tactile rock detail, restrained film grain, optical aerial photography,
immense scale, rich but not neon. Wide landscape 16:9 composition, clear broad shapes that will also
read when translated into ASCII. The center is cinematic scenery; no people, no buildings, no
devices, no text, no logos, no watermark, no border, no collage. It should feel like a striking
film frame with warm coral/orange highlights and cool teal water, not a generic tropical stock photo.
