# SwitchRadio

A square switch and a round radio group in hairline lines.

## Provide

Id, label and caps line; radio items with a disabled flag.

## Use

For settings like 静默模式 and the line picker.

## States

- On: `signal` edge and knob.
- Focus-visible: ring on the track or mark.
- Disabled: `rule-strong` lines and `text-faint` labels.

## Don't

- Do not round the switch: only the radio mark and status dots are round.
