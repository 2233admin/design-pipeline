# ScaleMarker

The scale marker: a 10px tick for a person beside a bracket for the structure, with both sizes and the ratio.

## Provide

Two sizes with their names and the ratio (e.g. 1.7 m 测深员 / 3,400 m 竖井宽度 / 1 : 2,000).

## Use

Once per screen that shows structure: it states the 1 : 10+ scale contrast in numbers instead of drawing a figure.

## States

- Static. On plates it sits on a `scrim` backing so the caption keeps 4.5:1.

## Don't

- Do not draw a person in its place.
- Do not shorten the bracket under 160px: its length against the tick is the point.
