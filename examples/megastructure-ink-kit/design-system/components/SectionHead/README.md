# SectionHead

A section head: number, a thin tracked title, its caps line and the giant depth numeral.

## Provide

Number, 中文 title (2–4 characters), English caps and the depth.

## Use

At the top of every site screen except the first.

## States

- Static. The depth numeral is `text-faint` at `display-depth` (88px+), the title `title-section` (40px, weight 300): the numeral is the loud part.

## Don't

- Do not set the title heavy: the voice is thin and quiet.
- Do not add a subtitle sentence; one short line in `body` at most below the head.
