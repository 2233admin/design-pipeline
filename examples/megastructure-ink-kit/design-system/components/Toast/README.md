# Toast

Toasts: `panel-raised` strips with a 2px left edge in `line`, `signal` or `alarm`.

## Provide

Kind, text, caps line.

## Use

Short confirmations and line-down notices; they leave after 5.2 s.

## States

- Info, ok (`signal`), alarm (`alarm`); close button in each.

## Don't

- Do not put actions other than close in a toast.
