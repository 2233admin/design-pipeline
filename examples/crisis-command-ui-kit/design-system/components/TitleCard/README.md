# TitleCard

Title card: the number line at half size and the title in heavy Mincho broken into an L, on black, paper or the red card.

## Provide

Number (第壹报), line one (one glyph), line two before and after the comma; ground (`--black`, `--paper`, `--red`); `--card-h` sets every size; `--column` sets the number vertically.

## Use

Chapter and report openers, the hero of a screen, cut-in cards in a film. Keep one per view; nothing else on it.

## States

- Static: a card has no hover of its own; inside a report index the card turns paper on hover.
- The comma takes half a glyph (`.cc-card__comma`).
- `--sx` compresses the title (never below 0.65).

## Don't

- Do not add pictures, rules or English to a card.
- Do not centre it: it weighs to the lower left.
- Do not recolour beyond the three grounds.
